# Mock Interview Reflection

*   **Interviewer:** Jane Doe (Peer / Senior Technical Lead)
*   **Interviewee:** Gloria Tago
*   **Date:** October 24, 2023

---

## What Went Well
*   **Structured Explanations:** I applied the STAR method successfully during behavioral questions, which kept my explanations highly concise and result-oriented.
*   **Domain Expertise:** I demonstrated strong intuition when explaining how data cleaning choices (such as median imputation) directly affected the outcomes of the "Small Farm Product" capstone analysis.
*   **Technical Articulation:** The explanation of sql JOINS and statistical p-values was clear, utilizing analogies that non-technical stakeholders could understand.

## Areas for Improvement
*   **Answering Under Pressure:** I tended to rush into answering technical SQL problems immediately instead of taking 10–15 seconds to outline my approach first.
*   **Pace:** I spoke a bit too rapidly when explaining complex parts of my data optimization pipeline, which made it harder for the interviewer to take notes.

## Concrete Practice Plan
1.  **The "Pause & Plan" Strategy:** For the next two weeks, I will practice taking a deep breath and spending 30 seconds outlining my response on a virtual notepad before speaking.
2.  **Mock Interviews:** Conduct 2 additional mock sessions focusing on live SQL coding tests and edge cases via platforms like Pramp or with classmates.
3.  **Speed Control:** Record myself answering sample technical prompts to actively train my speech pacing down to 130–150 words per minute.
